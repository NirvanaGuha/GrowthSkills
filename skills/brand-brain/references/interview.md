# Gap-Filling Interview

Run only after the scan, and ask **only** for fields the scan left missing or low-confidence. Never re-ask what's already known. Show the user a tight summary of what was found first, then batch the remaining questions (don't drip one at a time). Aim for the smallest set that reaches "minimum viable brand."

## Order of priority (ask top-down, stop when the brain is usable)

1. **Offer mechanics (the CTA-critical gap scans usually miss)**
   - Is there a free plan? (limits) · A free trial? (length, credit card required?) · A demo? · A guarantee / refund / "cancel anytime"?
   - What is the **primary conversion action** and its **destination URL**? Any secondary?

2. **ICP + awareness tendency** (if not already clear)
   - Who is the ideal customer (title, company shape, scale)? What pains/triggers make them act?
   - Where do most arrive on the awareness ladder (unaware → problem → solution → product → most-aware)? (Drives default CTA commitment.)

3. **Real differentiators**
   - What are the 3–7 true, defensible reasons to choose this over alternatives? (Confirm or supply — never invented.)

4. **Voice**
   - Three adjectives for how it should sound. Any banned words/phrases or punctuation rules?

5. **One real proof point**
   - A stat, rating, or named customer you can cite — or an explicit "none yet."

6. **Positioning frame** (if missing)
   - In one line, the competitive frame every piece of copy should reinforce.

## Minimum viable brand (don't finish Bootstrap without these)
- What it is + who it's for (ICP) + awareness tendency.
- The core differentiator(s).
- Offer mechanics that gate CTAs + the primary destination.
- 3 voice adjectives + any banned words.
- One real proof point (or "none yet").

Everything else (full proof vault, lexicon, visual identity, notes) can fill in later via `brand-brain refresh`.

## Tone of the interview
Specific and fast. Ask like an operator scoping a project, not a form. Offer your best guess from the scan and let the user confirm/correct ("From what I found, your ICP looks like mid-market retention owners — right, or narrower?") — confirmation is faster than open questions.
