## The problem with "AI for marketing" today

You've tried the prompt packs. Two weeks later they're abandoned. Three reasons:

- **They forget your brand.** Every prompt starts from zero. You re-paste your voice, your ICP, your offer, your banned words — every single time.
- **They're islands.** A headline tool that doesn't know your positioning. An email tool that can't reach your proof. Nothing connects, so nothing compounds.
- **They sound like AI.** Generic frameworks, hedge-everything copy, the tics a reader clocks in half a second.

A pile of prompts isn't a system. This is one.

## What makes it a system

**One brand brain. Every skill reads it.**
Run the bootstrapper once, pointed at your site, and it builds a reusable brand brain: voice, ICP, positioning, offer mechanics, real proof, banned words. All 269 skills load it automatically. You never re-explain your brand again.

**Skills call skills.**
The campaign builder calls the ad-copy writer, which calls the CTA generator, which reads your offer from the brand brain. The blog engine pulls verified stats from your proof vault. Composition, not copy-paste — so the output is coherent across your entire funnel, not a patchwork.

**Opinionated frameworks, baked in.**
Not "write a blog post" — write it on a real editorial framework, with the SERP gap filled, the differentiation mapped, the internal links planned. Every skill encodes how a senior operator actually works, so a solo founder or a junior hire ships senior-grade output.

**Quality gates, not vibes.**
Every creation skill has a paired reviewer that grades the draft against your brand brain before it reaches you. Output you can ship without a rewrite.

## How it works

1. **Install** the library (below).
2. **Bootstrap your brand** — `brand-brain-bootstrapper` scans your site and builds your brand brain in about five minutes plus a few questions. Multi-brand and agency-ready.
3. **Run any skill** — by name or a plain-language trigger ("write me a launch campaign for…"). It loads your brand, does the work, and saves the artifact to your project.

Your brand data lives in your own directory — never inside the skills — so updates never wipe it, and one brain serves every project you work in.

## Built to a standard

- Every skill verified against a strict contract: it loads the brand, composes its siblings, and never invents proof.
