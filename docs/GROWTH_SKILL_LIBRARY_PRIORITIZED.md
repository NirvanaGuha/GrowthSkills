# Growth Skill Library — De-duplicated & Prioritized Build List

*The shippable cut of the ~1,144-skill master blueprint. Near-duplicates merged across layers, weak entries cut, every survivor scored by buyer-value vs. build-effort and sorted into a build order.*

Last updated: 2026-06-19

---

## What happened

- **Started from:** ~1,144 entries across 35 layers (intentionally over-inclusive).
- **After per-cluster dedup:** 578 skills.
- **After global cross-cluster reconcile:** **412 canonical skills.**
- **Cut/merged in reconcile:** 214 merged, 63 cut.
- **Spine (must-have backbone):** 133 skills marked ⭐.

> **Merge & cut philosophy:** The heaviest merges hit the cross-cluster measurement and ops stacks. Tracking-plan/taxonomy auditing existed in four layers (L10, L11, L14, L21) and collapsed to two canonicals (taxonomy builder/auditor + GTM tag/pixel QA); UTM builders existed in three layers and collapsed to one; CRM hygiene/dedup/CSV-cleaning spanned L12/L14/L21 into one runner; the Salesforce SOQL+report+hygiene trio became one. Funnel drop-off analysis (L2 GA4 Gap Spotter, L9, L10 Mapper, L11 Activation) folded into a single L9 analyzer. Reviewer/QA, summarizer, and sequence duplicates were rampant and consolidated per family: meeting-notes/agenda (L17/L19/L23), pre/post-mortems (L15/L19/L23), stakeholder-status writers (L19/L23 + ticket translator), headline generators (L5/L6/L16), repurposers (L5/L6), win-back and onboarding sequences (L7/L11/L27/L31), vendor renewal/cost briefs (L24/L25), headcount/org-design (L24/L25), and the lead-scoring/PQL/routing trio. I cut the lowest-value over-granular entries and the cross-layer twins absorbed into suites while preserving the full L35 flagship orchestrator spine (all 16) and the distinct must-have backbone skills in every layer. No two survivors are near-duplicates; channel-specific specs (LinkedIn/TikTok/Reddit ad builders, ESP-specific builders) were kept only where platform syntax is genuinely load-bearing, otherwise unified under platform-parameterized suites (ESP/MAP Platform Builder absorbed Marketo and Klaviyo×Shopify; Shopping Feed Auditor absorbed the eComm feed optimizer).

**How to read the tables:** **Value** (1-5) = how much a paying marketer wants & uses it. **Effort** (1-5) = build effort (1 = a prompt; 5 = needs integrations/data/automation). Tiers are ranked by priority = value×10 − effort×4 + spine bonus. Re-sort by Value or Effort to match your build capacity.
